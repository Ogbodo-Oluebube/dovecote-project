How Dovecote works

A dovecote is where messenger pigeons were kept before they were sent out. Ours does the same for notifications.

From request to delivery

  1. You create a notification: channel, recipient, message, and an optional send time.
  2. Dovecote saves it as pending and replies immediately. Nothing is sent yet.
  3. A background worker picks up due notifications, highest priority first, and marks them processing.
  4. If delivery works, the notification becomes sent. If not, it is retried after a delay.
  5. After the last attempt fails, it becomes failed and waits for you to retry it.

The retry schedule

Each notification gets 3 attempts. The wait doubles after every failure.

EventWhat happens next
Attempt 1 failsRetry in 10 seconds
Attempt 2 failsRetry in 20 seconds
Attempt 3 failsMarked failed

Retrying a failed notification by hand gives it 3 more attempts.

Channels

Email is sent through an SMTP inbox. SMS uses a simulated carrier that fails some of the time, so you can watch retries happen. In-app notifications appear in the recipient’s Dovecote inbox.

Statuses

Pending is waiting for its turn. Processing is being sent right now. Sent was delivered. Failed ran out of attempts. Cancelled was stopped before it was sent.
